Tax Burden Distribution Analysis

Competition project on tax redistribution in fictional Fiskalia

This analytical report examines how income sources and tax regimes influence redistribution outcomes. The model compares labor, business, and capital taxation and evaluates reform impact by decile.

Scope

  • Revenue calculations for progressive PIT, flat PIT, and capital gains tax.
  • Tax wedge and effective rate analysis across income deciles.
  • Two reform scenarios with fiscal and social impact estimates.
