Tax Burden Distribution Analysis

Fiscal Analysis of Fictional Fiskalia

This project was prepared for an analytical competition. The topic was the distribution of tax burdens in the fictional state of Fiskalia, based on micro-tax data. The objective was to examine how different income sources and taxation regimes affect redistribution outcomes. The analysis was built in RStudio using an .Rmd report and rendered to .pdf.

Labor Tax Progressivity

The effective tax rate increases with income, confirming the progressive nature of labor taxation. Higher deciles bear proportionally larger tax burdens than lower-income groups.

Business Income Taxation

Progressivity appears limited for business income. The tax wedge rises quickly to around 20% and then flattens, suggesting near-proportional taxation in higher-income groups.

Proposed Tax Reforms

Scenario A

Lower bottom rate + higher top rate

  • New scale: 10% / 45%
  • Lower burden for lower incomes
  • Estimated revenue drop: ~5.8%

Scenario B

2.5k PLN deduction + higher top rate

  • New top rate: 45%
  • Deduction: 2,500 PLN
  • Estimated revenue drop: ~4.9%
